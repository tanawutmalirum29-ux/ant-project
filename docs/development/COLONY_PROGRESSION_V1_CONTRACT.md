## 2. Research engine — A-owned `src/core/research.js`

Export immutable `RESEARCH_CATALOG` in the table order and `Research`. Constructor accepts `{rooms,foraging,colony,isDefeated,catalog=RESEARCH_CATALOG}`, where `isDefeated()` is CEO-wired to actual `game.defeat || game.colony.queen?.alive===false`. **Public API exactly:**

- `research.snapshot()` — freshly copied, deeply detached/immutable serializable DTO; no leaking mutable Sets, actual rooms, pickup references, or exposed setters.
- `research.start(techId)` — atomic physical purchase, returns `{ok:true,runId}` or `{ok:false,error:{code}}`, not exceptions for expected validation.
- `research.cancel(runId)` — exact one-active-id all-or-nothing physical refund with the same result envelope.
- `research.tick(simTick)` — one progression unit at most for each **new** safe positive integer simulation tick while alive; never wall time.
- `research.hasCompleted(techId)` — O(1) pure boolean; effects read this, do not cache permanent additive buffs on ants/rooms.
- `research.refundableFor(roomId,type)` — nonmutating per-source-room/per-resource exact active refund liability (0 if none).
- `research.held` and `research.spent` (read-only copies via snapshot and stable numeric internal ledgers) record real units, one accounting credit each.

**Engine DTO required:** `{schemaVersion:1,revision,completedIds,technologies,active,inventory,held,spent,refundPreview}`, all detached. `technologies` is exactly six ordered rows `{id,title,description,effectLabel,prerequisiteIds,missingPrerequisiteIds,cost,totalTicks,status,missingResources,canStart}`, where engine `status` is `LOCKED|AVAILABLE|QUEUED|RESEARCHING|COMPLETED`; `AVAILABLE` means prereqs complete (not necessarily funded). `active` is null or `{runId,techId,status,startedTick,progressTicks,totalTicks,cost,pickups}`, with `status` `QUEUED|RESEARCHING`, copied pickups `{sourceRoomId,type,amount}`. `inventory` is the *physically accessible spendable* amount by type rather than spoofable aggregate (C facade may also display total stored separately); `missingResources` is deficiency for each resource across eligible physical depots. `canStart` additionally needs no running project, living queen, sufficient physical stocks. `refundPreview` is current active cost by type if exact original depots can safely refund; otherwise null. `revision` monotonically increases for actual visible state/progress transitions.

**Error `code` vocabulary:** `INVALID_TECH`, `ALREADY_RESEARCHED`, `PREREQUISITE_LOCKED`, `INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES`, `RESEARCH_ACTIVE`, `NO_ACTIVE_RESEARCH`, `STALE_RUN_ID`, `REFUND_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `GAME_OVER`, `INTERNAL_INVARIANT_ERROR`. No unexpected state mutation on any unsuccessful return. Validate death → tech ID/already researched → one slot → prereqs → physical available on start.

**Purchase:** eligible sources exactly ready+connected queen/food/water depots permitted by `Rooms.storageSlots(type)`. Scan stable room ordering, preflight **all three** types across actual `room.stock` before deducting ANY stock or aggregate `foraging.inventory`; commit a deterministic complete pickup plan together, or rollback entirely (including if an invariant changes). Never consume resource-node `amount`, in-flight cargo, `node.reserved`, or spoofed legacy aggregate as purchase money. A's successful purchase debits real stock/inventory and increases `research.held`, NOT `spent`; claims lock identical free source-room capacity to permit exact cancellation. A may never call `Rooms.reserveMaterials` as a substitute for all-three-type atomic preflight.

**Timing:** one project at a time. `start` begins at zero progress and status `QUEUED`, with `startedTick=game.clock.ticks`. First subsequent actual engine tick calls `tick(game.clock.ticks+1)`, moves to `RESEARCHING` and progress 1. Repeated identical simTick, bad tick, speed 0 (no FixedStepper invocation), or dead queen cannot award extra progress. On exactly `progressTicks===totalTicks`, transfer held→spent once, add completed tech ID once, empty `active`. Effects are consumed by all gameplay systems from the **following** fixed tick only. A re-attempt after cancellation uses a fresh strictly increasing `runId`.

**Refund:** `cancel(runId)` before completion refunds **100%** of held project materials to the exact `sourceRoomId/type` identified in pickups, even if source is temporarily disconnected (still physically present); no interest/progress discount, no partial refund, no change in `spent`. Preflight all original sources, capacity and ledgers before writing ANY refund. If a source is deleted, room shrunk, or capacity would overflow, fail `REFUND_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, preserve project and held escrow, never silently mint into queen. An already completed project is no longer cancellable and yields **0 refund**. No stale-ID or duplicate cancellation effects. World restart makes a new empty Research engine/ledger; after queen death with `pauseOnDefeat=true` or false, `active` and its `held` remain **terminal frozen** (no auto-refund, no progress, no new manual start/cancel). This deliberately supersedes the nonfinal initial #160 auto-refund suggestion; QA #164 and B #162 reviewed this case.

**Exact end-of-tick owner integration:** CEO constructs Research after existing rooms/lifecycle/combat and sets `colony.research`, `foraging.research`, `rooms.research`, `lifecycle.research`, `combat.research` and `lifecycle.combat`. Insert an explicit research system **after Combat and `colony.tickAmbient`**, but **before any ordinary `Game.onTick` subscriber and the always-LAST `MilestoneTracker.observe`**; do not reorder baseline engine consumers. Calling with `this.clock.ticks+1` is correct because `FixedStepper` increments `clock.ticks` *after* the step callback returns. Guard `game.defeat/queen.alive` before advancing; even offline `pauseOnDefeat=false` may not research after death, including fatal tick. Preserve current queen-defeat and queued-egg cleanup behavior. No action may mutate a terminal Game.
